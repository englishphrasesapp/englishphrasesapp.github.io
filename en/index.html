FAQ

Still have questions?

Is the app free?

Yes. English Phrases is free to use. On Android and iOS, discreet ads help keep the project going. The optional rewarded ad "Support the app" grants a 1-hour ad-free window.

Does it work without internet?

Studying works offline: phrases, translations, and voice stay on your device. Internet is needed for mobile ads, AI model downloads, and the optional usage statistics and crash reports.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up or login. Each installation keeps its own phrases and preferences on the device.

Does my data leave my device?

Your phrases, translations, prompts, and AI results stay on the device. Mobile ads use Google AdMob, and the optional statistics and crash reports use Firebase; both are explained in the privacy policy.

Is local AI required?

No. You can use the app with phrases only, build a prompt for an external assistant, or download a model to generate phrases locally. The download is optional and can be removed later.

Which languages does the interface support?

There are 14 interface languages, including Portuguese, English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Arabic, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi, Indonesian, and Vietnamese. The phrase translation field accepts any language.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, use the cache cleanup and "Restore defaults" options. To delete everything, uninstall the app (or clear the site data in your browser). For optional data already sent to Google, contact us by email.
