Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Summary

  • The app does not require an account and does not send your phrase content to our servers.
  • Phrases, translations, prompts, AI results, preferences, voice cache, and logs stay on your device (or in browser storage on the web).
  • On Android and iOS, the app shows Google AdMob ads. We request non-personalized ads (NPA), do not use ATT/IDFA, and remove the AD_ID permission on Android; even so, Google may process data for delivery, frequency, reporting, and fraud prevention.
  • Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics) and crash reports (Crashlytics) are optional, start disabled, and never include phrase content.
  • AI models are downloaded from Hugging Face; after download, local generation works offline.
  • You can review consent in the app and contact us at englishphrases.app@gmail.com.

1. Who is responsible

The controller for the data described in this policy is English Phrases ("we", "us"). Our privacy contact is englishphrases.app@gmail.com. We do not maintain a formal Data Protection Officer (DPO); this contact handles privacy requests.

This policy applies to the English Phrases app on Android, iOS, macOS, Windows, Linux, and the web, and to the website englishphrasesapp.github.io.

In this first phase, the app is publicly distributed only for Android and iOS. Desktop and web versions are not publicly available; when released, this policy will continue to apply to them.

2. Data stored on your device

The app is designed to work without an account and with your content on the device. The following stays local:

  • phrases, translations, IDs, creation dates, and the "shuffled" flag;
  • usage preferences such as language, theme, voice, speed, volume, automatic mode, filters, and table page;
  • FLAC audio cache and the voice diagnostic log;
  • downloaded AI models and the engine optimization cache;
  • import and export files you choose.

This data is not sent to our servers. On desktop it stays in the app's data directory; on the web it stays in browser storage (Drift with sqlite3.wasm, Cache API/OPFS). Uninstalling the app, clearing browser data, or using the app's cleanup options removes it. The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, or contacts; the file picker only accesses the file you choose. Notification permission may be requested for background features.

3. Ads (Android and iOS only)

On Android and iOS, the app uses Google AdMob to show banners, full-screen ads (interstitial), and the opt-in rewarded ad "Support the app". Desktop and the web do not show ads.

Google's SDK may process data such as IP address, device and app information, device and advertising identifiers when available, ad interactions (impressions and clicks), diagnostic data, and approximate location inferred from the IP address.

The app requests non-personalized ads (NPA), does not use ATT/IDFA, and removes the AD_ID permission on Android. This reduces personalization, but it does not mean zero collection: Google may still use data for frequency capping, aggregate reporting, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Ad consent is managed by the UMP (Google User Messaging Platform) where required. If you decline, the app does not request ads and keeps working; you can review the choice in the app's privacy dialog. The rewarded ad is optional and grants a 1-hour ad-free window; watching again restarts the window. Ads use a maximum PG content rating.

The legal bases are consent where required and, as applicable, legitimate interest or compliance with legal obligations for security, measurement, and fraud prevention. Learn more in Google's privacy policy.

4. Usage statistics and crash reports (optional)

On Android and iOS, the app offers two independent consents in its privacy dialog: usage statistics (Firebase Analytics) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). Both start disabled and only send data after you enable the corresponding option.

  • Firebase Analytics: usage events such as tabs opened, phrase actions, search and filters, import/export, prompt, automatic mode, local AI, voice, language, theme, and the ads funnel. Events use fixed values, counts, and ranges.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports with stack traces, device and app information, and the state at the time of the error.

We never send phrases, translations, prompts, generated JSON, custom scenarios, search text, file names, voice names, phrase IDs, or error messages. Firebase is not initialized on desktop or the web.

You can disable each option at any time in the privacy dialog. Collection stops, but data already sent may remain with the providers for their retention periods. The legal basis is consent. See Firebase privacy.

5. On-device AI and system AI

The app can generate phrases with models running on the device. The Gemma 3 1B, Gemma 4 E2B, and Gemma 4 E4B models are downloaded on demand from public repositories on Hugging Face. The download contacts Hugging Face and may expose your IP address, user agent, and technical connection data to that provider under its policy.

After download, prompts, phrases, and results stay on the device and are not sent to our servers. The models take approximately 557 MB to 3.4 GB, plus the optimization cache, and can be removed in the app.

System AI — Gemini Nano on Android, Apple Foundation Models on iOS 26+, Phi Silica on Windows Copilot+, and Gemini Nano in Chrome when available — is provided by the operating system or browser. Processing by the manufacturer follows its policies; the app does not send the content to our servers.

The prompt for an external assistant flow is manual: you copy the text and paste it into the service you choose. Processing by that service follows its provider's policies.

6. Voice

The native voice is provided by the system TTS engine (for example, Google, Apple, espeak-ng/spd-say on Linux, or the Web Speech API in the browser). Audio is generated on the device, but the system provider may have its own policies. The embedded voice synthesizes audio locally with sherpa-onnx, and the FLAC cache stays on the device. The app does not record microphone audio.

7. Website

This website is static and does not use tracking cookies, Google Analytics, Firebase, or third-party pixels. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log IP addresses and technical access data to operate, protect, and diagnose the service, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you email our contact channel, the message and any data you include are processed by the email provider (Google/Gmail) and by us to respond to your request.

8. Sharing

We share data only when necessary and with:

  • Google (AdMob, Firebase, and Google Play services), for ads, statistics, and crash reports where applicable;
  • Hugging Face, for AI model downloads;
  • system providers, for TTS and native AI, depending on your device and settings;
  • GitHub, for hosting this website;
  • public authorities, when required by law or to protect rights.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share your phrase content with advertisers or AI providers; prompts and results from local AI stay on the device. Links to third-party sites follow those sites' policies.

9. Retention

  • Local data: stays on the device until you delete it (cache cleanup, "Restore defaults", uninstall, or clearing browser data).
  • Ads and Firebase: for the periods defined by Google; we do not control those periods.
  • AI models: until you remove them in the app.
  • Voice log: local, rotating after 1 MB.
  • Contact emails: for as long as needed to respond and comply with legal obligations.

10. Security

Local data is protected by the operating system sandbox and, on the web, by browser storage. Model downloads use HTTPS and, on native platforms, the app verifies the file size and SHA-256. No system is 100% secure; keep your device updated and protected.

11. Your rights (LGPD and GDPR)

You may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, deletion, portability, information about sharing, withdrawal of consent, objection, and review of decisions. For local data, you can exercise these rights directly in the app (cache cleanup, "Restore defaults", or uninstall).

For data processed by Google, Hugging Face, or GitHub, we can guide and forward your request; some aggregated or anonymized data may not be deletable. To exercise your rights, email englishphrases.app@gmail.com. We may ask for information to confirm your identity and will respond within the legal period.

In Brazil, you may complain to the ANPD. In the EU/EEA, you may complain to your local data protection authority. In the US, we do not sell personal data; you can request access and deletion at the same email.

The app does not make automated decisions with legal effects. Shuffle and automatic mode are study features only.

12. Children and teenagers

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. In Brazil, we observe the special protection of children (under 12) and teenagers. If a parent or guardian believes a child provided data, contact us so we can guide deletion. Parents and guardians should supervise use; ads use a maximum PG rating.

13. International transfers

Google, Hugging Face, and GitHub may process data outside Brazil, including in the United States. Where applicable, these providers use contractual and technical safeguards for international transfers. See their policies for details.

14. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or the law. The date at the top indicates the current version. Material changes will be announced on this website and, where appropriate, in the app. Continued use after an update means you are aware of the new version.
